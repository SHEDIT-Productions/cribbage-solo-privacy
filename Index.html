Privacy Policy

Cribbage Solo: Hand Builder
SHEDIT Productions, LLC
Effective date: September 2, 2026
Last updated: September 2, 2026

This policy explains what information Cribbage Solo: Hand Builder (“the app”) collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. The app is published by SHEDIT Productions, LLC (“we”, “us”).

The short version

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud storage. Your game progress stays on your device. The only data collection that occurs is by Google AdMob, which serves the advertisements in the app.

Information we collect

We collect nothing. The app does not gather your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any other personal information. There are no user accounts and no way to sign in.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game data locally on your device so it is there when you return. This includes your current game, cumulative score, statistics, Daily Challenge streak, Joker count, unlocked card designs, and your sound, vibration, and animation preferences.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it and it is not transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app removes it permanently, and it cannot be recovered.

Advertising

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob, a service operated by Google LLC. To serve ads, AdMob collects certain information from your device, which may include:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (a resettable ID used for advertising purposes)
  • Device information such as model, operating system version, and language
  • General location inferred from your IP address (country or region level, not precise location)
  • Information about how you interact with advertisements

Google may use this information to show you personalised advertising. This collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google’s own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can review how Google uses information from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your advertising choices

You can limit or reset advertising personalisation at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings › Google › Ads. You can delete or reset your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation.
  • iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region with similar requirements, the app will ask for your consent before showing personalised advertisements. You may change or withdraw that consent at any time from within the app.

Rewarded advertisements

The app offers optional rewarded video advertisements. Watching one is entirely voluntary and grants an in-game Joker. Declining has no effect on your ability to play. The same AdMob data practices described above apply to these advertisements.

Children

The app is not directed to children and is not intended for them. You must be at least 13 years old to use the app, or at least 16 if you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom.

We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will address it.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information, because we do not collect any. The only third party that receives data is Google AdMob, as described in the Advertising section above.

Data retention

Because we do not collect data, we do not retain any. Game data stored on your device remains there until you delete the app or clear its data. Information collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google’s policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information held about you, or to object to its processing. Since we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to provide or delete. For data collected by Google AdMob, please refer to Google’s privacy policy and controls linked above.

Security

Game data is stored in your device’s private application storage, protected by your device’s own security. As we do not transmit or store your data on any server, there is no server-side data to secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app changes — for example, if we add a new feature or service. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will be communicated within the app where practical. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
